Chapter 7 · The command palette

The rule run-kit is built on: every action is registered in the palette. It's the discovery mechanism — when you don't know how, ⌘K (⇧Ctrl+K on Win/Linux) and type.

the chords worth memorizing
⌘K / ⇧Ctrl+Kthe palette — the only chord you truly need
⌘,Settings — in the desktop app. Mac browsers reserve ⌘, for their own preferences: there, ⌘K → settings. Win/Linux: ⇧Ctrl+,
⌘/ / ⇧Ctrl+/Shortcuts tab — every binding; app bindings are remappable, tmux bindings are locked

Takeaway: this tour showed you a fraction of the surface. ⌘K is how you find the rest.
