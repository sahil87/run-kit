Sidebar Material Studies

Design study (260915 discussion) for giving the desktop sidebar a surface of its own — a gray material distinct from the terminal, the way macOS window chrome (sidebar, toolbar) sits on a gray material while the content well keeps its own color. Today every desktop surface — sidebar card, terminal tile, top bar, status bar — paints --color-bg-primary = palette.background, separated only by the 6px stage ground (bg-bg-inset) and the 55%-alpha card border. Authorities: docs/specs/themes.md § UI Color Derivation (every UI color derives from the 22-color palette — the gray must too), visual-design.md § Two-family chrome vocabulary (floating cards on the stage vs the attached frame), shell.tsx (the stage grid). Every frame below is generated from a real theme palette; the toolbar drives the big mock in § B.

B · The live mock

One frame, every control in the toolbar. Variant picks the structure; seam picks how the sidebar meets the terminal; ΔL is the OKLab lightness step between the gray and palette.background (sign by theme category — up on dark, down on light; recessed goes down on both); chroma kept is how much of the background's own hue survives in the gray (0 = neutral macOS gray, 100% = the theme's tint lifted as-is). re-base row tints toggles whether the family tints blend into the new surface (proposed) or still into palette.background (what ships today — watch the RUNKIT/FABKIT bands and the selected row grow a halo when it is off). flyout card drops a row-hover card so you can judge popup occlusion on the new ground.

D · Deriving the gray per theme

The gray cannot be a hex: 70 palettes ship, and the Themes spec derives every UI color from the palette. Four candidate formulas on 14 themes. Each cell is the gray beside that theme's background with the WCAG contrast between them (1.0 = identical). A step under ~1.15 does not read as a different surface; over ~1.6 the sidebar starts to look like a different app.

  • Today's tokens are asymmetric by category. On dark themes card (lighten 8%) is a visible ΔL 0.06–0.08 step while inset (darken 5%) is ΔL 0.005–0.011 — invisible. On light themes it flips: card (darken 3%) is ΔL 0.02, barely a surface, while inset (darken 6%) is ΔL 0.045 and plainly visible. So the stage ground shows on light and vanishes on dark; the cards separate on dark and merge on light. A sidebar material needs one token that is the same visible step in both categories — which neither existing token is.
  • OKLab ΔL is perceptually even across all 70 and across categories — the same visible step on ayu-dark, nord and solarized-light. (WCAG ratios are shown small for reference only: the +0.05 flare term compresses them near black, so a plainly visible 0.06 step reads as 1.14:1 on default-dark and 1.20:1 on default-light.) Chroma is scaled, not zeroed: at 0 (pure neutral) the gray fights a tinted terminal (solarized's teal, ubuntu's aubergine, tomorrow-night-blue) — the chrome looks pasted on. At 100% the gray inherits the theme's tint and stops reading as "gray" on those same themes. ~35% keeps kinship without the tint.
  • mix(fg 6%) is the shortcut (one blendHex); it tracks foreground hue, so warm-on-cold themes (gruvbox-dark's cream text on gray) go beige. Rejected.
  • Recessed (ΔL down on dark) clamps at black for the handful of #000-background themes (synthwave, tomorrow-night-bright, dark-pastel) and has almost no room on ayu-dark / night-owl — the clamp cells. Lifting always has room.

Open questions

  • Boards / server route. The board page and the 900px server column are floating cards on the same stage. Under #2 they stay palette-colored (content), which is consistent — but the server route's tiles are chrome-like lists, not terminal content. Decide per route when #2 is drafted.
  • Light themes. A gray sidebar beside a white terminal is exactly Finder; the risk is the other way — bg-inset wells inside the gray reading as holes. Check solarized-light and gruvbox-light specifically (both have cream backgrounds whose neutral-gray step looks cold).
  • Row-color legibility. Family tints were tuned at 14/22/40% against the page ground; on a lighter dark-theme gray the 14% rest band loses a little pop. Re-measure the WCAG border guardrail (3.0) after re-basing — the guardrail already handles it but the visual weight may want 16/24/40.
