Act 4 · It needs you

The promise of delegation: you're interrupted only when a decision actually needs you.

how an agent reaches you
tour-worker needs you
🔔 run-kit
tour-worker: who's the audience — exec or engineer?

The row badge is always there; the notification reaches your device if you enabled the bell. Agents can never grab your screen — they signal, you decide when to look.

the loop — do it now
  1. Spot the badge on the worker's row (or tap the notification).
  2. Click the row — its window opens, the question is right there in its terminal.
  3. Type your answer and press Enter. That's the whole unblock.
  4. Come back to me — the worker finishes on its own, presents its page, and pings you when it's done.

This is the skill. Delegate → get interrupted only when needed → unblock in one line → receive the result as a page. Everything else in run-kit exists to make this loop scale.
