Screen Break Crack Realism Studies

Phase-2 mock for the screen-break Easter eggs, variant C (glass + dead-pixel lines, chosen 2026-09-16) at the slow 12 s pacing. The glass break: tapered radials that branch and sometimes stop mid-screen · partial arcs instead of full rings · a frosted crushed zone of micro-cracks around the hole · the shipped jagged hole and nine shards. The LCD layer: 5–8 dead-pixel rows/columns in magenta / cyan / green / white / yellow popping in from crack tips to the screen edge. Everything is SVG strokes and transforms, nothing samples pixels; this is the mechanism, not a picture of it. Ink (liquid-crystal bleed) is kept behind a toggle, off, for comparison only.

4. Decisions

Variant C chosen (2026-09-16): glass realism plus dead-pixel lines ships; the ink layer (liquid-crystal bleed blobs and tongues) is rejected — the weaker cue on dark themes and it blots terminal text on light ones. The Ink toggle stays in this page as the comparison artefact it is.

Reference-photo rework rejected: an organic lobed hole with a bright pulverized ring of ~90 glass chips, 36 hairline radials, and glass dust was tried side by side and judged "looks worse". The shipped 18-vertex jagged hole and the sector shards stay unchanged.

Pacing: the slow 12 s flight is approved — a slow start so the crack web stays visible much longer, with the shipped beats (flash, hole, shards, creature, heal) kept in the same order.

Deferred: an easter_eggs settings-registry bool (default true) is a separate follow-up change, not part of this port.
