2 · Marker ink — four options, dark and light

Same tree everywhere: rows carry a label color (amber · green · blue · purple; two rows uncolored) with today's rest/selected family tints, a waiting halo on iron-heron, and a closed-PR glyph on old-crane — the two places the status pyramid already spends hue.

Ink pickers re-renders every dark/light frame in §2–§4 live. Light is pinned at #d97706 (your pick); dark at #f59e0b.
Light ink — keep the hue, drop the lightness
The dark ink #f59e0b is hue 38°. The earlier light pick #b45309 is hue 26° — that's the red cast. Holding hue at ~35° and darkening until a graphic clears 3:1 on #f8f9fb gives #c97400 (3.3:1) or #c46800 (3.7:1); the chosen light ink is #d97706 (3.0:1) — a shade lighter, judged by eye against the frames above. #f59e0b itself measures 2.0:1 on light by the WCAG formula — but a saturated orange on a white page reads louder than the number suggests, so the frames above now use it as the starting point and the picker decides. Token: --color-marker-ink = #f59e0b dark / #d97706 light, paired the way --color-signal-yellow already is (#facc15 / #b07d02).

3 · The marker well — giving the empty part a shape

A 7px stripe under a 22px slab reads as a gap, not as "one third". The fix is to draw the well the fill lives in, so 0 → ⅓ → ⅔ → full is legible on every row. Three ways to draw it, C1 ink, dark and light.

4 · T4 — the gap between the well and the status dot

Today the well's right edge is at x=26 and the dot's leading edge at x=30 (pl-[30px]): a 4px gap, and the waiting halo (3px spread) closes it to 1px, so the well and the dot fuse into one cluster. Three spacings, T4 ink, dark and light; the name column shrinks by the same amount the gap grows.

Gap recommendation: drop the well's 4px inset — well at x=0–22, dot stays at 30. The desktop shell already puts the sidebar card on a 6px inset ground behind a 1px card border, so the row's x=0 is 7px from the app edge; the stripe's STRIPE_EDGE_INSET = 4 dates from the pre-card full-bleed sidebar and now just duplicates that breathing room. Removing it yields the 8px gap without moving the dot, the name, or any constant but one. The well then anchors to the card edge exactly the way the row tint, hover and selection fills already do. Mobile drawer caveat: there the sidebar is flush to the viewport, so the well would touch the screen edge — acceptable (the row tints already do), but worth one look on a phone.

Recommendation: C1 — one fixed, theme-paired ink; no marker color picker. The marker is now an instrument (mode × stage), and an instrument should look the same on every row so the eye learns "amber shape = my marker" and reads the shape, never the hue. Label color stays exactly what it is — the row's grouping tint, picked from the card — and the two never compete: the label lives in the row's tint and the group header, the marker in a 22px strip. C0 (today's inherit-the-label-hue) has one real defect: uncolored rows get a gray stripe that is nearly invisible. C2 (semantic hue per mode) double-encodes nicely but spends red and blue, which the dot and PR glyph already own — red would mean "blocked" on the strip and "closed PR" 200px to the right on the same row. C3 (a small user palette for the marker) is a third free axis on a 3×3 — 36 states — and inherits both problems.
