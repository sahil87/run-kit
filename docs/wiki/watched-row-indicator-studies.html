Watched-Row Indicator Studies

Where does "watched by the operator" live on a sidebar window row? The C6 clock rung shipped a green ◉ beside the StatusDot without passing through the StatusDot vocabulary (docs/specs/status-pyramid.md, the marker 3×3 studies). This study puts that glyph back on the grid it skipped, tests five alternatives against the full hue × shape × overlay matrix, and records a recommendation. Every dot here mirrors status-dot.tsx at true size (7px, 9px flagged), with 3× insets.

The pyramid's rule for anything that is not journey or liveness: flags are additive overlays — never a hue, never a shape. "Watched" is a flag. The wuiu intake called it greenfield because grep ◉ returned nothing; the grid above says it was never greenfield — every channel already had an owner, and the glyph took the one thing (a colored dot) the row could not afford a second of.

Read across the green ring row: A0 doubles the hue; A1 and B1 add a concentric shape the eye must separate from the bullseye two rows up; B3 and C1 leave the dot exactly as the pyramid draws it. 3 · Recommendation

Ship B3, the underbar, and give it a register. Three decisions travel with it:

  • Neutral ink only. The bar is text-text-secondary in every state; never accent-green. Hue on the row belongs to journey (dot) and the PR glyph. Stale = the note-stale opacity-50 treatment plus a dashed bar, so staleness is not encoded in opacity alone.
  • A fifth register, opr. The dot is documented as a pure function of the four register lines on the flyout card and the PANE panel. An overlay with no register breaks that. Add opr watched · apply · tick 2m ago below PR, absent when unwatched — the same degrade-to-absent the WatchedLine already uses; the two lines merge into one.
  • The bar is decoration, the dot stays the tap target. No new hit area; the accessible name grows one clause ("building — at rest — watched") on the dot's existing aria-label, and WatchedIndicator as a separate role="img" goes away.

Decision · the well stays human-owned

Raised and closed in the 260910 discussion: could the auto chevron stand in for "watched", making a dot overlay redundant? No. Marker modes are fully manual — a person declares them, nothing derives them — and that is the point of the axis. So status may never depend on the marker mode, and the marker may never carry status. Watched-ness therefore needs its own derived channel on the row, which is what the underbar is. The E1-plus-chevron path is closed.
