Logo Design Studies

The hexagon-cube brand mark in every state it ships in — static, loading, at rest in the brand crumb, under the hover sweep, and as an icon — rebuilt from the shipped geometry, CSS and math (logo-spinner.tsx, globals.css, icontint.go, generate-icons.sh) rather than from screenshots, so every demo below is live and pixel-faithful. Consolidates the three earlier logo studies (§5); none of their artifacts remain in the repo — the 2026-04 page's variants are replayed in §1. 260910 discussion, glow toned 260912.

Segments are indexed 0–5 clockwise from the upper-right edge — the order of BORDER_SEGMENTS in logo-spinner.tsx, which is also the polygon order in icon.svg. The static fills are the single source of truth for the rest pattern: the hover sweep derives lit-trio membership from staticFill === LIT_FILL rather than restating the index set. All five grays are literals, not theme tokens — the mark renders identically in dark and light (see the ladder below). ViewBox 7 10 50 44, pre-computed coordinates, no transforms.

Every shipped size, on both app grounds

Sizes are the literal size props in use. On the dark ground (#0f1117) the dark trio sits at ≈1.3:1 against the background and all but vanishes — the ring reads as three lit edges plus the cube, lit from the upper right, which is the intended drawing. On the light ground (#f8f9fb) it inverts: the dark trio jumps to ≈12:1 and the lit trio fades to ≈1.8:1, so the mark reads as lit from the lower left. That inversion has never been studied (§6). Below 14px the cube faces merge into one gray disc and only the ring carries the shape.

Stop transition — the 2026-04 study, replayed

The first logo study (assets/logo-animation-preview.html, 2026-04-04 — removed 2026-09-12 once this page replayed it) compared two chase variants × three ways to stop. A3 fade-to-original shipped: animation: none plus a 0.5s ease-out transition on both opacity and fill, so the uniform lit ring settles back into the two-tone rest pattern. A1 and A2 both left the ring uniformly #b4b4b4 — the rest pattern never came back — on top of A1's snap. D pulsed the cube faces (2.4s, staggered 0.8s) as well; rejected, and the faces have stayed static in every treatment since.

2 · At rest — the brand crumb and the sidebar brand row

Hover the brand in any mock. The top-bar crumb is LogoSpinner size={20} loading={false} plus the RunKit wordmark at text-xs inside the link-crumb box (28px, 1px border, hover brightens border and text); hidden below sm, where the sidebar brand row (size 18, bold, tracking-wide) takes over as the home affordance. Both anchors carry rk-brand-glitch: a one-shot RGB-split text-shadow (300ms, steps(2), ±2px) and color: accent-green for the hover — the one deliberate exception to "hover on controls is hue-free": identity, not a control state. 3 · Brand hover sweep — detach, orbit, land

hover or click to play
900ms · 3 laps · toned-white glow · re-trigger while running is ignored
Scrub p by hand, and drag the glow slider to tone the blob — it drives every sweep on this page (big demo, filmstrip, brand mocks, the C card). Shipped: #c8c8c8 (200) since 2026-09-12; 260803 shipped pure #ffffff, which read too harsh at crumb size. The lit gray is #b4b4b4 (180), so the peak sits only just above rest — what makes the blob read is the lit half dimming to ≈47 while it flies, not the peak itself. The frames at p = 0 and p = 1 compute to exactly the rest fills — the landing and the rest state are the same event, which is the whole design goal: the eye must not be able to tell "the animation stopped" from "the lit segments reset".

Filmstrip

h is the fractional head position (segment units, clockwise), s the settle envelope — 1 at both ends (rest), 0 mid-flight (glow). Detach over the first 10%, land over the last 22% (from p = 0.78). Three whole laps park the head on segment 0, the lit trio's center, so the blob's landing spot IS the rest pattern's position — a fractional lap count would land off-center and make the restore a visible snap no easing could hide.

ease   = 1 − (1 − p)³                                  h = (ease · 3 · 6) mod 6          (continuous)
s      = max( 1 − smoothstep(p / 0.10),  smoothstep((p − 0.78) / 0.22) )
d_i    = min(|h − i|, 6 − |h − i|)                      gauss_i = exp(−d_i² / (2 · 0.85²))
rest_i = 1 if i ∈ {5, 0, 1} else 0                     B_i = gauss_i · (1 − s) + rest_i · s
bright = mix(GLOW, #b4b4b4, s)   GLOW = #c8c8c8          fill_i = rgb(mix(#2a2a2a, bright, B_i))
driver: requestAnimationFrame; inline `transition: fill 0.5s` suspended for the flight and restored on landing;
        prefers-reduced-motion ⇒ skipped in JS (no CSS gate line); the p = 1 frame is exact, the literal hex restore is a safety net

Rejected, for the record — hover each

Two shipped treatments preceded the sweep: the kickstart spin (260703-5ilm — the whole logo, four turns in 600ms, launched at full speed so the launch reads as flicker) and PR #362's refinement that spun only the ring (720°, transform-box: fill-box so the pivot is the hexagon center, not the viewBox origin). Both moved geometry — a second motion grammar beside the chase's illumination. The 2026-08 mock then went through a stepped head (floor(ease·N) — whole-segment jitter, and the last step lands only on the final frame, so the ring parks then snaps), a gray glow on an overlay (invisible over the lit half's own #b4b4b4), and an accent-green glow — rejected outright: "green is not the answer. Needs to be white only." White is the only color that reads over the lit half, and it keeps the Brand row's green cue on the wordmark's glitch where it already lives. On 2026-09-12 the pure #ffffff was toned to #c8c8c8 — same math, a far softer peak; the slider above shows the range.

Instance accent — the colorize ramp (260722-eo8e)

luma  = (299·R + 587·G + 114·B + 500) / 1000            (BT.601, integer)
pixel ≤ 24 luma → untouched (the #0f1117 plate stays dark)
else  channel = (accent_channel · luma + 90) / 180, clamped 255     — #b4b4b4 (luma 180) maps to the accent itself
grays → #b4b4b4 100% · #888888 76% · #737373 64% · #545454 47% · #2a2a2a 23% of the accent

Exact port of icontint.go's integer math (TintSVG substitutes these five fills; TintPNG runs the same ramp per pixel), so the swatches above are what the Dock, Cmd-Tab and the tab favicon show for each owned family at the default shade — the hexes are frozen from the default-dark theme's OKLCH rendering, since a Dock icon is theme-independent. Won a four-option mockup (colorized logo ✓ · tinted background plate · accent ring · badge dot) because it stays legible at Dock sizes. Descriptors outside the ten owned families (e.g. 7, 2+5) render the stock gray. That mockup was not committed (§5).

6 · Recorded decisions, and what is open

Decision (visual-design.md § Design Decisions)One line
Brand logo motion is state-based, not a transform spinone motion vocabulary — segment illumination — across loading and hover; no geometry moves; white (toned #c8c8c8 since 2026-09-12), never green
Landing IS the rest stateinteger lap count + a settle envelope that is 1 at both ends; the literal hex restore is a safety net, not the mechanism
Chase stagger is negativesteady state from frame one; keyframes and timing untouched
Brand glitch is the hue exceptionhover on controls is hue-free; the brand's green is identity, not control state
Reduced motion: zeroed, not removedCSS zeroes the chase; the JS sweep skips itself; the rest state is the reduced state — no CSS gate line for JS treatments
Icons are derived, tint is request-timeone canonical SVG; PNGs generated; the accent tint is computed per request from settings (Constitution II), never persisted
